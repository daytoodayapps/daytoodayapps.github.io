Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Tally Counter.

Is Tally Counter free to use?
Yes! Tally Counter is completely free to download and use with full core counting functionality, widgets, sound effects, and daily resets. We also offer an optional Pro subscription to remove all ads and support ongoing development.
Is an iPhone / iOS version coming to the Apple App Store?
Yes! The iOS version is currently in development for Apple iPhone and iPad users and will be released on the Apple App Store soon. You can use the Android app on Google Play right now.
Does the app require an internet connection?
No! All counters and count histories work 100% offline. You can count anywhere — in airplanes, remote hiking trails, or subways without internet.
How does counting with physical volume buttons work?
When Volume Key Counting is enabled in settings, pressing Volume Up increments your active counter, and Volume Down decrements it. This lets you count discreetly in your pocket or during workouts without looking at the screen.
How can I backup or transfer my counters?
You can tap "Export to Excel (CSV)" inside the app settings to save a full backup file. You can restore this file on any new device using the "Import Counters" feature.
