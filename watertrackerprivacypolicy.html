Privacy Policy for AquaTrack: Water Tracker

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Welcome to AquaTrack ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a transparent understanding of how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application AquaTrack: Water Tracker (the "Application").

By installing or using AquaTrack, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device (Personal Hydration Data)

AquaTrack is designed with a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture. The core data you generate while using the Application is stored locally on your device using a private database (Room SQLite database) and local encrypted preferences (DataStore). This includes:

  • Daily hydration targets and water intake goals
  • Water intake logs, timestamps, container choices, and notes
  • Custom hydration plan schedules and reminders
  • Profile preferences (display name, reminder intervals, theme selection)
Your Data Stays With You: We do NOT transfer, upload, or sell your personal water intake logs or user profile data to external servers or third-party cloud storage.

B. Biometric Authentication Data

If you enable the Biometric Lock feature:

  • The biometric verification (Fingerprint or Face Unlock) is performed entirely by the Android Operating System using standard Android Biometric APIs (androidx.biometric).
  • AquaTrack never accesses, stores, or transmits your biometric samples, templates, or raw fingerprint/facial data.

C. Automatically Collected Information & Third-Party Advertising

To support the development and maintenance of AquaTrack, the app includes advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process technical device identifiers and app usage data in compliance with Google's policies and applicable privacy laws. This data may include:

  • IP address (used to infer general coarse location for regional ad delivery)
  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or other device identifiers
  • Device make, model, operating system version, and system language
  • Diagnostic data, app crash reports, ad views, and interaction metrics

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please consult:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Terms

2. Device Permissions Used and Why

AquaTrack only requests permissions necessary to deliver the features you choose to use:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch and display banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob and check connectivity.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API level 33) and higher to deliver scheduled hydration reminders to help you reach your daily water intake goal.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide subtle haptic feedback when logging water or completing milestone goals.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Enables optional device-level app locking to keep your hydration logs private on your device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data Retention: All your hydration logs and schedules remain stored in the local app storage for as long as the application remains installed on your device.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual water logs directly within the app history. If you uninstall the application or clear app data via Android Settings > Apps > AquaTrack > Storage > Clear Data, all locally stored logs, settings, and database entries are permanently removed from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

AquaTrack does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose productivity and health utility. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party ad providers through our app, please contact us so appropriate measures can be taken.

5. Third-Party Links and Services

The Application displays advertisements that may contain links to third-party websites or services. If you tap on a third-party advertisement, you will be directed to that third party's site or app. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
